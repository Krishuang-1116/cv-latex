## Why only ~47 applicants in 2 weeks (your question)
Not a red flag on the role — a structural combination that shrinks the qualified-AND-interested pool:
1. **Location = West France, not Paris.** ~80% of French data talent is in Paris. A regional (Nantes/Rennes) role has a far smaller local pool → far fewer applicants. This is the #1 driver.
2. **ESN / consulting model.** Many strong data engineers self-select AWAY from the régie/staffing model (placed at clients), so the pool shrinks again.
3. **Brand-new/nascent data practice at a non-data-brand company.** eXalt isn't known as a data employer; "the data offer is just arriving." Candidates who want a mature data team or a product company skip it. Low employer-brand pull for data.
4. **Hard 4-year gate on paper.** Filters juniors out; experienced AEs in West France are scarce and often already placed. Small "meets 4 yrs + wants regional ESN + available" intersection.
5. Net: it's a genuine, decent role with LOW competition because of WHERE it is and WHO it appeals to — not because it's bad. Low competition = better odds IF you're open to the location + ESN model.
